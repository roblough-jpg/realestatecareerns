Ask ten agents how their brokerage markets their listings on social media and you'll get ten answers. Some brokerages post everything. Many post a few featured listings, or leave it all to the agent. It's worth knowing before you sign.

## Why does it matter who posts your listings?

- **Sellers notice.** Showing a seller exactly where their home was marketed helps you win the listing and keep the seller happy.
- **Consistency.** When posting depends on someone finding time, some listings get missed. A system doesn't forget.
- **Reach.** A brokerage page reaches a different audience from your personal page. Together they cover more ground.

## How it works at Century 21 Optimum Realty

Every listing is posted automatically to our brokerage [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/C21OptimumRealty) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/c21optimum/) pages, with branded graphics and a written caption:

- **When it goes on the market,** including a video post for every new listing
- **When there's an open house**
- **When the price changes**
- **When it sells**

You don't have to request it, design anything or remember to do it. That leaves you free to share the posts with the people who know you, which is where they do the most good.

**Seller reports.** You also get a report to share with your seller, so they can see how their listing has been marketed. It's an easy update to send, and it shows the work you're doing.

**Coming next.** We're setting up every agent with their own account, where they'll see their own reports. The goal is to post listings straight to each agent's own social media pages too.

## Questions to ask any brokerage

- Do you post every listing, or only some?
- Which events: new listing, price change, open house, sold?
- Which platforms, and how quickly after the listing goes live?
- Who creates the graphics and captions?
- Is there a cost to me?

Add these to your [10 questions to ask a brokerage before you sign](/blog/questions-to-ask-a-brokerage-before-you-sign/).
