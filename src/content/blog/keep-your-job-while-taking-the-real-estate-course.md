Most of the agents I've hired didn't quit their jobs to take the course. They studied at night and on weekends, and kept their paycheque until they were licensed. That's usually the smart way to do it.

## Can I work full-time and take the real estate course?

Yes, if you take the online version. NSAR's online Salesperson Licensing Course is self-paced, with no live sessions, and you have six months to finish. You're assigned a faculty advisor you can email with questions.

The classroom version is different. It runs on Zoom on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays from 9:00 to 4:00 for 16 weeks. That's hard to fit around most day jobs.

## How do I fit the course around my job?

- **Book fixed study time.** Two or three evenings a week and a weekend morning beats cramming when you feel like it.
- **Set a finish date well inside the six months.** Life gets in the way, so leave yourself room.
- **Study for the exam as you go.** You have 12 months after the course to pass, and two attempts. Don't let the material go cold.
- **Tell the people at home.** You'll be less available for a few months, and it helps to have them on side.

## When should I leave my job?

Not the day you pass the exam. Before you give notice, have these in place:

- **Your brokerage chosen** and your licence application signed. Use these [10 questions to ask a brokerage](/blog/questions-to-ask-a-brokerage-before-you-sign/).
- **Savings to cover several months.** You're paid only when a deal closes, and that can be 30 to 60 days after an accepted offer.
- **A plan for your first 90 days.** The free [90-day plan](/90-day-plan/) shows what to do week by week.

## Can I stay part-time in real estate after I'm licensed?

Some people do, at least at first. It comes with real trade-offs, and not every brokerage accepts part-time agents. We do at Century 21 Optimum Realty. I cover it in [part-time real estate in Nova Scotia](/blog/part-time-real-estate-agent-nova-scotia/).
