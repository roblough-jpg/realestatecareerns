The course is the first big cheque you write on the way to a real estate licence. It's also the one people most often ask me how to cover. Here's what it costs, what doesn't work, and what does.

## How much is the real estate licensing course in Nova Scotia?

The Nova Scotia Association of REALTORS (NSAR) runs the Salesperson Licensing Course. Both prices include tax and the textbook.

- **Online, self-paced:** $3,418.86. You have six months to finish.
- **Classroom on Zoom:** $3,988.86. It runs 16 weeks, Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays, 9:00 to 4:00.

After the course, the licensing exam is $100 plus tax ($114), paid to the Nova Scotia Real Estate Commission. You have to pass it within 12 months of finishing the course.

## Can I get a student loan for it?

No. The course isn't approved for federal or provincial student loans. NSAR also requires payment in full when you register, so you need the money in hand before you start.

## Practical ways to pay for it

- **Savings.** The simplest option, and it means you start your career without new debt.
- **A line of credit.** Lower interest than a credit card. Have a plan to pay it down, because your first commission may be months away.
- **A family loan.** Common, and fine, as long as everyone agrees in writing on when it gets paid back.
- **Help from a brokerage.** Some brokerages offer reimbursement or a signing bonus to offset education costs. Read the conditions, especially any clawback if you leave early. At Century 21 Optimum Realty, I'm open to talking about course costs with anyone who's serious about joining us.
- **Keep your current job while you study.** The online course is self-paced, which lets you keep earning. I cover that in [can I keep my job while taking the course](/blog/keep-your-job-while-taking-the-real-estate-course/).

I'd avoid putting the full amount on a high-interest credit card. The interest keeps adding up through the slow first months.

## Don't budget for the course alone

The course is only the first cost. Before you earn a dollar you'll also pay for the licence, NSAR and CREA memberships, and errors and omissions insurance. Then you need money to live on until your first deal closes.

Use the [licensing cost calculator](/tools/licensing-cost-calculator/) to add up your own number. Then add six months of living expenses on top.

## What should I do before I register?

- **Pick online or classroom** based on your schedule, not just the price difference.
- **Check the English requirement.** NSAR requires a CELPIP-G score of 7 or higher in all four parts before you register, with no exceptions.
- **Talk to a brokerage early.** Your broker signs your licence application, and some brokerages help with costs. Use these [10 questions to ask a brokerage](/blog/questions-to-ask-a-brokerage-before-you-sign/).
- **Read the [90-day plan](/90-day-plan/)** so you know what the first months after licensing look like.
