I built BrokerageFlow because I was tired of deal files living in five places: email, a shared drive, a phone and a paper folder. I wanted a system that doesn't just store the paperwork but reads it and keeps the deal moving. Here's what it does for our agents.

## What is BrokerageFlow?

BrokerageFlow is the deal and compliance system agents at Century 21 Optimum Realty use every day. Every deal has one file. The documents, the dates, the contacts, the FINTRAC records and my review all live together in that file.

## What does it do for an agent?

- **It reads your forms.** Upload or email an Agreement of Purchase and Sale, counter offer, amendment or waiver. BrokerageFlow identifies the Nova Scotia form, pulls out the dates, price, deposit and parties, and fills your checklist. Amendments update the deal automatically, and handwritten changes are flagged for a second look.
- **Deadline reminders.** Deposit, conditions, sale of buyer's property and closing dates are tracked from the documents, and you're reminded before each one. Ask for your own reminder too: "Email me 4 days before the SOBP date."
- **Email documents in.** Send documents to the BrokerageFlow email address. It matches the email to the right deal by address, MLS number or client name. A new listing or offer starts a draft file, and anything unclear asks you once.
- **Send documents from your phone.** Pick the lawyer, lender or clients, tick the documents and tap Send. It goes out under your name, and replies come back to your own inbox.
- **Ask AI.** Ask about your deal: "What's still missing?" or "Has the deposit come in?" Tell it what to send, and it prepares the email with the right people and documents. You review it and tap Send. Type or talk.
- **Contacts that fill themselves in.** Your clients are added from the brokerage agreement. Lawyers come from the office directory, with their assistant copied automatically, and the mortgage broker can be included on every email. Save any contact to your phone, or export them to your CRM.
- **Commission statements.** Built from the deal, addressed to the right brokerage or lawyer, and emailed from the file.
- **Trust deposits.** Deposits are recorded against the deal with receipts, so you can see at a glance whether the deposit is in.
- **Broker review in the same place.** I review your file in BrokerageFlow, so you know what's missing before it becomes a problem.
- **Every offer attempt kept.** If a buyer writes on one property, then another, the history stays together instead of starting over.
- **Annual AML training built in.** Video modules and quizzes, with a certificate when you pass.

## A deal in BrokerageFlow

Your buyers' offer is accepted. You forward the signed package from Authentisign, and BrokerageFlow files it, reads the dates and adds your clients. Two days before the deposit is due, you get a reminder.

The inspection comes back, so you forward the report from the driveway and say "send it to the lawyer and the mortgage broker." After the waiver, it's filed and the conditions are marked done. A week before closing, the office makes sure your commission statement has gone out.

## What doesn't it do?

Offers are still written and signed in WEBForms and Authentisign, the tools you already know. BrokerageFlow doesn't replace them. It picks up from the signed paperwork and handles everything around it.

## Why does it matter?

FINTRAC expects identification and records on every deal, kept for at least five years and produced within 30 days if asked. When that lives in one organized file, an audit is a non-event. More importantly, you spend less time chasing paperwork and more time with clients.

For the rules themselves, read [FINTRAC for Nova Scotia agents](/blog/fintrac-for-nova-scotia-real-estate-agents/).
