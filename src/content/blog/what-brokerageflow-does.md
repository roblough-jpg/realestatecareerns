I built BrokerageFlow because I was tired of deal files living in five places: email, a shared drive, a phone and a paper folder. Compliance is part of every deal, and I wanted it to be the easy part. Here's what it does for our agents.

## What is BrokerageFlow?

BrokerageFlow is the deal and compliance system agents at Century 21 Optimum Realty use every day. Every deal has one file. The documents, the FINTRAC records and my review all live together in that file.

## What does it do for an agent?

- **One file per deal.** Signed documents, identification and records sit with the deal they belong to, not in your inbox.
- **Email documents in.** Send documents to the BrokerageFlow email address and they land in the right file.
- **Ask AI.** Ask a question about a form or a compliance rule and get a fast answer, right inside the file.
- **Broker review in the same place.** I review your file in BrokerageFlow, so you know what's missing before it becomes a problem.
- **Every offer attempt kept.** If a buyer writes on one property, then another, the history stays together instead of starting over.
- **Annual AML training built in.** Video modules and quizzes, with a certificate when you pass.

## What doesn't it do?

Offers are still written and signed in WEBForms and Authentisign, the tools you already know. BrokerageFlow doesn't replace them. It's where the finished paperwork and the compliance around it are kept.

## Why does it matter?

FINTRAC expects identification and records on every deal, kept for at least five years and produced within 30 days if asked. When that lives in one organized file, an audit is a non-event. More importantly, you spend less time chasing paperwork and more time with clients.

For the rules themselves, read [FINTRAC for Nova Scotia agents](/blog/fintrac-for-nova-scotia-real-estate-agents/).
