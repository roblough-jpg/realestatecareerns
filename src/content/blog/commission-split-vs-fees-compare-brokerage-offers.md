Every brokerage leads with its best number. One offers a bigger split, another a lower monthly fee, a third a cap. The only fair way to compare them is to run your own year through each offer and see what you keep.

## What's the difference between a split and a fee model?

- **Split model.** The brokerage keeps a percentage of each commission you earn. Monthly fees are usually lower, so a slow month costs you less.
- **Fee model, including 100% plans.** You keep most or all of each commission and pay higher fixed fees every month, whether or not you close a deal.
- **Hybrids and caps.** Many offers mix the two: a split until you reach a yearly cap, plus some fixed fees.

None of these is automatically better. It depends on how many deals you close and how steady they are.

## How do I compare two offers?

1. **Estimate your year honestly.** How many deals, and what's your average commission per deal before the brokerage takes anything? Use last year's numbers if you have them. If you're new, be conservative.
2. **List every cost at each brokerage.** Split, cap, monthly fees, technology, franchise or royalty fees, per-deal fees, insurance, signs and marketing.
3. **Run the numbers for a good year and a slow year.** Fixed fees hurt most when deals are slow.
4. **Compare what you keep, then what you get.** Training, leads, admin help, marketing and compliance support all have a value. Paying for them yourself costs money and time.

## A worked example

These numbers are made up to show the method. Use your own.

| | Brokerage A | Brokerage B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commission model | 70% to you | 100% to you |
| Monthly fees | $100 | $600 |
| Per-deal fee | $0 | $300 |
| 10 deals at $8,000 each | You keep $56,000 − $1,200 = **$54,800** | You keep $80,000 − $7,200 − $3,000 = **$69,800** |
| 4 deals at $8,000 each | You keep $22,400 − $1,200 = **$21,200** | You keep $32,000 − $7,200 − $1,200 = **$23,600** |

On these made-up numbers, B wins in both years, but by far less in the slow one. Change the fees or add a cap and the answer can flip. That's why you run your own numbers.

## Questions to ask before you sign

- What do I pay in a month when I don't close anything?
- Is there a cap, when does it reset, and what fees continue after I cap?
- What's included: signs, marketing, technology, admin help, training?
- Who reviews my files and handles compliance, and is there a fee for it?
- What does it cost to leave?
