# Post format

1. `answer` in the frontmatter: two or three sentences that fully answer the title question. AI search quotes this.
2. One short intro paragraph.
3. H2 sections phrased as the questions people search (use the AnswerThePublic wording).
4. A "Common questions" FAQ of three to five items in the frontmatter.
5. Sources in the frontmatter for every fact taken from elsewhere.
6. `draft: true` until Rob approves the pull request.
7. Description under 160 characters, with the main search phrase in it.
