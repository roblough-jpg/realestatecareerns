# Rules: what posts must never do

1. Promise or imply an income. Explain how commission works and cite public figures (Job Bank), never "you can earn $X".
2. Name or criticize another brokerage, or compare C21 Optimum to a named competitor.
3. State a licensing fact that isn't in facts.md, or one marked TO VERIFY, without Rob's sign-off.
4. Copy or paraphrase NSAR course material or exam questions. Practice quiz questions are written fresh.
5. Rewrite a page that already exists on optimumrealty.c21.ca. Link to it instead (see links.md).
6. Invent statistics, quotes, testimonials or agent stories.
7. Use client reviews without written permission.
8. Publish without Rob approving the pull request.
