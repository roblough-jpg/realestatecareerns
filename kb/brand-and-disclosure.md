- Every page shows "Presented by Century 21 Optimum Realty" in the header, and the footer names the brokerage, says it is licensed in Nova Scotia, and says "Each office is independently owned and operated." Never remove these.
- The site is not NSREC, NSAR or any government body, and must never look or sound like one. The footer says so and links to nsrec.ns.ca and nsrealtors.ca.
- REALTOR® is a CREA trademark. Use "realtor" in plain prose for the job; use REALTOR® only for CREA's membership meaning, with the ® symbol.
- Rob's tools (BrokerageFlow, Ask AI, List Launch): describe what agents get, never the software behind it. Write about a feature only once it is live.
- No AI-generated faces or voices presented as real people.
